CHICKEN CURRY

HOME

DESCRIPTION

Chicken curry is a popular dish featuring tender pieces of chicken simmered in a savory, spiced gravy made from onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and ground or whole spices

INGREDIENTS

  • chicken
  • cooking oil
  • onions
  • tomatoes
  • ginger garlic paste
  • green chillies
  • yogurt or coconut milk
  • water or chicken stock
  • salt
  • turmeric powder
  • kasmiri mirch
  • coriander powder
  • garam masala
  • cumin powder
  • bay leaf
  • coriander leaf

STEPS

Prep the base

  1. Heat 3 to 4 tablespoons of oil or ghee in a large pot or pan over medium heat
  2. Add the whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamoms, cloves, and cumin seeds) and let them sizzle in the hot oil for a few seconds until fragrant

    3. Sauté the Onion

  3. Add the finely chopped onions and a small pinch of salt
  4. Sauté on medium-high heat for 8 to 12 minutes until the onions turn golden brown and caramelized

    5. Add Aromatics and Tomatoes

  5. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the raw smell disappears
  6. Add the chopped tomatoes and green chilies. Cook until the tomatoes turn soft and mushy

    7. Add the Spices

  7. Reduce the heat slightly and add turmeric powder, red chili powder, and coriander powder
  8. Stir and fry the spices for about 1 to 2 minutes (a process called bhuno) until the oil begins to separate from the masala paste

    9. Cook the Chicken

  9. Add the chicken pieces into the pan
  10. Turn the heat up to high and sauté the chicken for 5 minutes until it changes color and turns pale or lightly browned

    11. Simmer and Build Gravy

  11. Pour in warm water (about 200–250 ml; avoid cold water as it can make the chicken tough). Stir well and bring the mixture to a gentle boil
  12. Cover the pot with a lid, lower the heat to a simmer, and cook for 15 to 20 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through

    13. Finish and Garnish

  13. Remove the lid, sprinkle in the garam masala powder, and stir well. Check the salt and adjust to taste
  14. Let it simmer uncovered for 2 more minutes to thicken the gravy
  15. Turn off the heat and garnish generously with freshly chopped coriander leaves

There you have it

eat it warm with either roti, butter nan or rice
